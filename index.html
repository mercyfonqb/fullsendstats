How points work

POINTS=(FFA WINS×2.0+TEAM WINS×1.0+RANKED WINS×1.5)×× WINRATE÷10

  • Points are awarded only for: rated FFA games, rated TEAM games, rated RANKED games, strictly within our clan.
  • HVN (Humans vs Nations), solo games, custom lobbies and tournaments are not included.
  • Only games played with the [FSC] tag count. Wear it, win, and promote the clan.

*Different game modes have different coefficients. This is intended to encourage players to participate in FFA (Free-For-All) and boost our clan’s standing on the global leaderboard. The overall contribution to the clan is very important.

Why a percentile-based system
  • ScalabilityThe system works just as well for a clan of 30 members as for one with 500, without restricting anyone’s playstyle.
  • Healthy competitionThe top role becomes an exclusive “elite club”, for example the top 2% of all members. In a clan of 500 that means the 10 strongest players, not just one, so reaching the top tier is a realistic and enticing goal.
  • A dynamic benchmarkTo stay in the top percentage, players must stay active and keep performing; otherwise newcomers can push them out.
